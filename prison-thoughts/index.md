### 囚思
<p align="center">
<iframe frameborder="no" border="0" marginwidth="0" marginheight="0" width=298 height=52 src="//music.163.com/outchain/player?type=2&id=417859631&auto=0&height=32"></iframe></p>

<p class="post">一往情深深几许？</p>

<p class="post">二心紧绑，三年孤依不言弃。</p>

<p class="post">炼金情，苦思忆，</p>

<p class="post">岁月多别离，</p>

<p class="post">人渐消沉天渐熄，</p>

<p class="post">思绪囚心屿。</p>
